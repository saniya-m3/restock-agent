# Linen & Lark — Returns & Refunds Policy

_Last updated: May 2026_

---

## Return Window

We accept returns within **30 days of delivery** for most items. The 30-day clock starts the day the carrier marks your order as delivered.

Items must be returned in their original condition: unworn, unwashed, unaltered, with all tags attached and in original packaging where applicable.

---

## Final Sale Items

**Sale items are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged.** This includes:
- Items purchased at a discount of 20% or more
- Items explicitly marked "Final Sale" at time of purchase
- Items purchased during sitewide sale events (e.g., Black Friday, End-of-Season)

If you are unsure whether your item qualifies, contact us with your order number and we'll confirm.

---

## Free Returns (US Orders)

- Orders with an item subtotal **over $75 USD** qualify for **free prepaid return shipping** within the contiguous United States.
- Orders under $75 USD: the customer is responsible for return shipping costs. We recommend a trackable shipping method — Linen & Lark is not responsible for lost return shipments.
- Alaska, Hawaii, and US territories: standard return policy applies, but free prepaid labels are not available. Contact us for options.

---

## International Returns

Customers outside the United States are responsible for all return shipping costs and any applicable customs duties or taxes. We recommend using a trackable courier.

International refunds are processed in USD at the exchange rate applied at the time of original purchase. Linen & Lark cannot be held responsible for exchange-rate fluctuations.

---

## Gift Returns

Items received as gifts can be returned for **store credit** within 30 days of delivery. The gift recipient may initiate the return — please have the order number or gift receipt handy. Original purchasers receive the refund to their original payment method only if they initiate the return themselves.

---

## Refund Timeline

- Refunds are issued to the **original payment method** within **5–7 business days** of us receiving and inspecting the return.
- Store credit is issued within 1 business day of return receipt.
- You will receive an email confirmation when your refund has been processed.
- Depending on your bank, the credit may take an additional 3–5 business days to appear on your statement.

---

## Defective or Incorrect Items

If you received an item that is defective or not what you ordered, please contact us within **14 days of delivery**. We will cover all return shipping costs and issue a full refund or send a replacement at no charge. Please include a photo of the issue when you reach out.

---

## How to Initiate a Return

1. Provide your order number and the email address used at checkout.
2. Our assistant will verify your order and confirm eligibility.
3. If eligible, a prepaid label (US orders over $75) or return instructions will be sent to your email.
4. Pack the item securely and drop it off at the carrier location within 7 days of receiving your label.

---

## Exchanges

We do not offer direct exchanges at this time. To exchange an item, return it for a refund and place a new order.

---

## Contact & Escalation

For complex issues — damaged shipments, VIP orders, or situations not covered by this policy — our support team can escalate to a human agent who will reach out within one business day.
